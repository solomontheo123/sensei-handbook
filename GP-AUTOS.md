# Project Overview

GP-Autos is an automotive marketplace designed to improve how people buy, sell, compare, and manage vehicles.

The platform combines:

- Automotive marketplace features.
- AI assistance.
- Vehicle intelligence.
- Trust systems.
- Digital services.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision of GP-Autos is:

> Build the most trusted automotive platform for Africa by combining marketplace technology, artificial intelligence, and transparent vehicle information.

The goal is not simply listing cars.

The goal is creating trust in the vehicle buying process.

---

# Problem

The automotive market has several challenges:

- Fake vehicle listings.
- Misleading information.
- Hidden vehicle history.
- Difficulty comparing prices.
- Lack of trusted inspection systems.
- Complex purchasing decisions.

GP-Autos aims to solve these problems.
